# KrishiGuard AI — Reviewed Agricultural Knowledge Base

This directory contains peer-reviewed and verified plant pathology references for KrishiGuard AI's **Farm Evidence Loop**.

## Authorities & Institutions Cited:
- **ICAR-IIHR** (Indian Council of Agricultural Research - Indian Institute of Horticultural Research)
- **TNAU** (Tamil Nadu Agricultural University Agritech Portal)
- **PAU** (Punjab Agricultural University Extension Bulletins)
- **ICAR-IARI** (Indian Agricultural Research Institute, Division of Plant Pathology)
- **UAS Bangalore** (University of Agricultural Sciences)

## Core Decision Rules:
1. **Never synthesize speculative treatment:** Recommendations must stem strictly from verified ICAR/TNAU protocols.
2. **Weather Correlation vs Proof:** Weather conditions are treated as an epidemiological *catalyst* (e.g., relative humidity > 90% and 12-22°C favoring Late Blight), not definitive diagnosis on their own.
3. **Safety Barriers:**
   - Strict ban on pesticide cocktail advice.
   - Ban on unauthorized antibiotic sprays (e.g. indiscriminate streptomycin use).
   - Clarification of viral vs fungal vs bacterial distinctions (e.g., never advising fungicides for TYLCV).
   - Clear escalation to Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or local agricultural officers when symptoms are ambiguous.
